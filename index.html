How the directory works

Girl Business means someone in the group owns it. Recommendation means a member has used them and vouches for them.

Adding someone? Search first to make sure they're not already listed, choose the right category and fill in everything you can. Incomplete, duplicate or inappropriate entries may be removed.

Blacklist lists businesses members have had bad experiences with. They never appear in the main listings, only in the Blacklist tab or when you search for that name.

Recommending? Please be honest. A recommendation isn't a guarantee, so use your own judgement when hiring someone.
